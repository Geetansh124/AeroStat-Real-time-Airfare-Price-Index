Reading the heatmap
■Fare below the (route, window) 90-day median — favourable for consumers on this corridor. ■Fare within ±3% of baseline — routine pricing behaviour. ■Fare above baseline — signals demand pressure or capacity constraint; feeds into AeroStat composite weight.

DGCA Back-test

AeroStat daily readings collapsed to monthly means and compared against DGCA's published monthly average domestic fare for the same sectors. Correlation, MAE, RMSE and MAPE quantify how faithfully AeroStat tracks the official benchmark at monthly cadence — while offering daily granularity in between.
AeroStat vs DGCA monthly averages
Both series indexed to Sep 2025 = 100
Divergence analysis

The strongest divergences occur in months where within-month volatility is high — DGCA's monthly mean smooths through spikes that AeroStat captures at daily cadence. This is a feature, not a defect: AeroStat surfaces the shocks that manual collection misses.

  • Oct 25 — Diwali corridor: AeroStat +6.8% vs DGCA +2.1%
  • Dec 25 — Year-end leisure: AeroStat +4.2% vs DGCA +3.9%
  • May 26 — Summer peak: AeroStat +5.1% vs DGCA +4.4%
Methodology notes

Collapsing daily → monthly · Simple arithmetic mean of daily AeroStat readings, weighted by trading days.

Sector alignment · DGCA reports 6 metro city-pairs; AeroStat basket includes all 6 plus 6 additional corridors. Back-test restricts to the DGCA intersection.

Base-period reconciliation · Both series re-based to Sep 2025 = 100 for comparability.
